This Privacy Policy explains how **Skndan** ("Skndan", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit **skndan.com** (the "Website"). By using the Website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to browse the Website. You may voluntarily provide information when you:

- **Contact us via email:** If you click an email link (e.g., balaji.m@skndan.com or privacy@skndan.com) and send us a message, we will have access to your email address and any information you include in your message.
- **Social media links:** If you interact with our social media profiles (GitHub, Twitter/X), those platforms may collect data according to their own privacy policies.

We do not operate contact forms, comment sections, or newsletter signups on the Website that collect personal data through form submissions.

### Automatically Collected Information

When you visit the Website, certain information is automatically collected:

**Microsoft Clarity**

We use Microsoft Clarity, a user behavior analytics tool, to understand how visitors interact with the Website. Clarity may collect:

- Pages visited and time spent on each page
- Mouse movements, clicks, and scroll behavior
- Browser type, operating system, and device type
- Referring URLs and general geographic location (country/city level)
- Session recordings (which do not capture text you type into form fields)

Clarity does not identify you personally and we do not use it to track individual users across the web. For more details, see [Microsoft's Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement).

**Server Logs**

Our hosting provider may automatically log standard information including your IP address, browser type, referring pages, and timestamps. These logs are used for security, diagnostics, and aggregate traffic analysis and are not used to identify individual visitors.

**Cookies and Local Storage**

The Website may use:

- **Theme preference:** A small localStorage entry to remember your light/dark mode preference. This contains no personal data.
- **Microsoft Clarity cookies:** Clarity sets first-party cookies for session tracking and user behavior analysis. These cookies do not store personally identifiable information.

You can disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that the Website will continue to function without cookies; only the analytics and theme persistence features will be affected.

## What We Do NOT Collect

We do not:

- Sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties
- Use tracking cookies for advertising or cross-site profiling
- Collect sensitive personal data (health, financial, biometric)
- Require or store user accounts, passwords, or login credentials

## How We Use Information

We use the limited information we collect to:

- **Improve the Website:** Clarity data helps us understand which pages are useful and how visitors navigate the site, so we can improve the experience.
- **Respond to inquiries:** If you email us, we use your email address to reply to your message.
- **Ensure security:** Server logs help us detect and prevent abuse or security threats.
- **Maintain the site:** Aggregate traffic data informs hosting and performance decisions.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.
