**ReadStack** is a reading habit tracker developed and published by **Skndan**. This Privacy Policy explains how ReadStack collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application.

By using ReadStack, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the App.

## Information We Collect

**ReadStack is designed as a privacy-first application.** Here is what we do and don't collect:

**Data stored locally on your device (we do NOT have access to this):**

- Your reading sessions, goals, streaks, and statistics
- Book titles, authors, notes, and reading progress you enter
- App preferences, themes, and notification settings

All of this data is stored **exclusively on your device** using local storage. We do not have servers that collect, upload, or store your reading data.

**Information we do NOT collect:**

- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- Location data or GPS coordinates
- Device contacts or calendar entries
- Browsing history or other app usage on your device
- Photos, media, or files from your device

**Automatically collected information:**

- **Crash logs & diagnostics:** If the App crashes, basic diagnostic data (device model, OS version, stack trace) may be collected through platform-provided services (Google Play Console / App Store Connect) to help us fix bugs. This does not include any personal or reading data.
- **Anonymous usage statistics:** We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (e.g., number of installs, session counts) through official app store analytics. This data cannot be used to identify individual users.

## How We Use Information

Since ReadStack stores all reading data locally on your device, we **do not access, use, or process** your personal reading data on any external server.

Any limited data available to us (crash reports, store analytics) is used solely for:

- Identifying and fixing bugs and stability issues
- Understanding aggregate usage patterns to improve the App
- Ensuring compatibility with future OS versions

We **never** sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any third parties for marketing purposes.

## Data Storage & Security

Your reading data is stored **locally on your device** only. We do not operate any backend servers or cloud databases that store your reading information.

**If you delete the App** from your device, all locally stored data will be permanently removed. There is no cloud backup service, so data cannot be recovered after deletion.

We recommend using your device's built-in backup services (iCloud for iOS, Google Backup for Android) if you wish to preserve your data across device changes. The App's local data may be included in these system-level backups depending on your device settings, but we do not control or access these backups.

## Third-Party Services

ReadStack may integrate the following third-party services:

**RevenueCat (In-App Purchases)**

- If you make a purchase or subscribe within the App, RevenueCat processes the transaction on our behalf.
- RevenueCat may collect an anonymous app user ID, purchase history, and subscription status to manage entitlements.
- No personal information (name, email, payment details) is shared with us — payment processing is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store).
- RevenueCat's privacy policy: [revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

**Apple App Store & Google Play Store**

- Distribution platforms may collect data as described in their respective privacy policies.
- Apple Privacy Policy: [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy)
- Google Privacy Policy: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

**Local Notifications**

- ReadStack uses on-device notifications for reading reminders and streak alerts.
- Notifications are scheduled and managed entirely on your device — no data is sent to any server.
- You can disable notifications at any time through your device's system settings.

## Children's Privacy

ReadStack does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the App stores all data locally and does not require account creation or transmit personal data, it is inherently safe for users of all ages. However, if you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information (e.g., via a support email), please contact us and we will promptly delete such information.

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

- **Access & Portability:** All your reading data is stored on your device. You can view, edit, or delete it at any time within the App.
- **Deletion:** Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data. If you've contacted us via email, you may request deletion of any correspondence.
- **Opt-out of notifications:** You can disable notifications through your device settings at any time.
- **No account to delete:** Since ReadStack does not require user accounts, there is no account deletion process needed.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, California, or other regions with specific privacy laws: because we do not collect or process your personal data on our servers, most data subject rights (such as access, rectification, and erasure) are exercised by you directly on your device through the App's interface or by uninstalling the App.
