Chimera AI is an Excel automation product built to bring AI-powered assistance directly into spreadsheet workflows. Instead of forcing users to move data into separate tools, the product works within the Excel environment and helps automate repetitive operations such as cleanup, transformation, interpretation, and structured output generation.

The project was built as a TypeScript-based Excel add-in integrated with OpenAI. Its real value lies in reducing the friction of manual spreadsheet work, especially in business settings where users spend hours editing rows, correcting patterns, generating formulas, and restructuring data for reporting or operations.

#### Tech Stack:

- <b>Add-in Logic:</b> TypeScript for building the application layer and spreadsheet interaction flow.
- <b>Spreadsheet Platform:</b> Excel add-in environment for embedding functionality into familiar user workflows.
- <b>AI Layer:</b> OpenAI for prompt-based automation, interpretation, and transformation assistance.
- <b>Office Integration:</b> VSTO / Excel plugin workflow for productivity-oriented desktop usage.

#### Key Features & Functionality:

- <b>Spreadsheet Automation:</b> Reduces repetitive tasks inside Excel.
- <b>AI-assisted Data Cleanup:</b> Helps normalize and organize messy sheet content.
- <b>Structured Output Generation:</b> Converts loosely formatted spreadsheet content into cleaner outputs.
- <b>Context-aware Assistance:</b> Works with spreadsheet context rather than as a detached chatbot.
- <b>Workflow Acceleration:</b> Helps users complete data-heavy tasks faster without leaving Excel.

#### Product Design Approach:

1. <b>Stay Inside Existing Workflows</b>
- The product avoids forcing users into a new platform.
- Excel remains the core workspace, lowering adoption friction.

2. <b>Automate High-friction Tasks</b>
- Focuses on the kind of repetitive spreadsheet work users do every day.
- Prioritizes utility over novelty.

3. <b>Blend AI with Control</b>
- AI suggestions need to feel reliable and reviewable.
- Users should benefit from speed without losing trust in the output.

#### Challenges Solved:

- <b>Manual Repetition:</b> Reduces repeated formatting and transformation effort.
- <b>Context Switching:</b> Keeps users inside Excel rather than moving them to external tools.
- <b>Productivity Bottlenecks:</b> Speeds up business workflows that depend on sheet-heavy operations.
- <b>Adoption Barrier:</b> Uses a familiar interface, which improves usability for non-technical users.

#### Why Chimera AI Stands Out:

Chimera AI is not just an AI demo layered on top of spreadsheets. It is a productivity product built around a real business environment where users care about time savings, output quality, and ease of use more than AI novelty. Embedding AI into Excel creates a practical bridge between traditional office workflows and modern language-model capabilities.

Chimera AI shows how AI becomes most useful when it lives inside tools people already trust. That makes it a strong portfolio project because it combines applied AI, product thinking, and workflow-aware software design.
