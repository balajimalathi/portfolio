Fedcorp is a banking application engagement focused on consumer and merchant experiences with UPI payment integration. The project involved building and maintaining transaction-critical workflows, improving stability, and ensuring that payment-related functionality worked reliably in a regulated digital banking environment.

The product sits in a space where users expect everyday convenience, but the underlying system must still meet strict standards for security, performance, and compliance. That made Fedcorp less about feature experimentation and more about dependable execution in production.

#### Tech Stack:

- <b>Mobile Application:</b> Flutter for consumer and merchant app experiences.
- <b>Payments Domain:</b> UPI integration for digital transaction flows.
- <b>Business Context:</b> Banking and fintech product delivery with RBI compliance expectations.
- <b>Maintenance Focus:</b> Stability improvements, production updates, and bug-fix releases.

#### Key Features & Functionality:

- <b>UPI Integration:</b> Secure transaction support for consumer-facing use cases.
- <b>Merchant App Maintenance:</b> Continuous updates to improve reliability and usability.
- <b>Banking-grade Stability:</b> Focus on performance, reduced crash risk, and safer releases.
- <b>Compliance-aware Development:</b> Product work aligned with banking and security expectations.
- <b>Production Support:</b> Ongoing fixes and refinements for live user environments.

#### Product Responsibilities:

1. <b>Payment Enablement</b>
- Integrated UPI flows into the banking application.
- Helped support secure and dependable digital transactions.

2. <b>Merchant Experience Support</b>
- Maintained the Fed Merchant app.
- Improved stability through regular updates and fixes.

3. <b>Production Reliability</b>
- Worked on bug resolution and release quality.
- Prioritized consistent user experience in a live banking product.

4. <b>Compliance-sensitive Delivery</b>
- Worked in a context shaped by RBI security expectations.
- Built with awareness of trust and operational correctness.

#### Business Impact:

- <b>Reliable Payments:</b> Stronger transaction workflows improve day-to-day usability.
- <b>Merchant Confidence:</b> Stable merchant tools support operational continuity.
- <b>Reduced Risk:</b> Better maintenance lowers the chance of user-facing issues in production.
- <b>Higher Trust:</b> Compliance-aware execution matters in digital banking products.

#### Why Fedcorp Matters:

Fedcorp is a valuable portfolio project because banking software is unforgiving. Users notice instability immediately, and financial workflows leave little room for weak engineering. Building in that environment shows the ability to handle trust-heavy product requirements with discipline.

The project demonstrates strength in fintech engineering, payment integration, and production reliability, especially in systems where compliance and stability are as important as feature delivery.
